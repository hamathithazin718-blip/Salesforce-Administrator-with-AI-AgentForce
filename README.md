# Salesforce Administrator with AI-AgentForce

A Salesforce Administration project integrated with AI Agentforce
to explore workflow automation and improve business efficiency.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on Salesforce Administration and the
integration of AI Agentforce to explore how intelligent agents
and automated workflows can support business operations.

The project follows a structured development process, including
brainstorming, requirements analysis, project design, planning,
development, testing, documentation, and demonstration.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand Salesforce Administration concepts.
- Explore AI Agentforce and its potential applications.
- Understand workflow automation in Salesforce.
- Learn about project planning, testing, and documentation.
- Gain practical exposure through a structured course project.

## 🛠️ Technologies & Platforms

- Salesforce
- Salesforce Administration
- AI Agentforce
- Workflow Automation
